# Cambridge O Level English Language 1123 — Syllabus Reference
*Source: Official syllabus PDF (2024–2026). Used to build Tahmeed's study sheet, July 2026.*

## Exam Structure
- 2 papers, 50 marks each, 2 hours each. Total: 100 marks.
- Grades: A* (highest) → E (lowest). Ungraded = below E.
- Available: June & November series. Target: October/November 2026.

## Paper 1 — Reading (50 marks, 2 hrs)
AO split: 80% Reading / 20% Writing

### Section A — Comprehension & Use of Language (25 marks)
| Q | Task | Marks | Text | AOs |
|---|------|-------|------|-----|
| Q1 | Comprehension (short answers) | 16 | Text A ~900 words, narrative, 20th/21st C | R1, R2 |
| Q2 | Use of Language (writer's effects) | 9 | Text A | R4 |

### Section B — Summary & Short Response (25 marks)
| Q | Task | Marks | Text | AOs |
|---|------|-------|------|-----|
| Q3a | Summary, continuous prose, MAX 150 words | 20 (10R + 10W) | Text B ~550–600 words | R1, R5, W2, W3 |
| Q3b | Short response on implicit meaning/attitude | 5 | Text B | R2 |

## Paper 2 — Writing (50 marks, 2 hrs)
AO split: 80% Writing / 20% Reading. Answer ONE from each section.

### Section A — Directed Writing (25 marks, compulsory)
- 1–2 stimulus texts (~400–450 words total)
- Write 250–350 words in one of: speech, email, report, letter, article
- Argumentative / discursive / persuasive register
- AOs: W1, W2, W3, W4, W5 (15 marks) + R3, R5 (10 marks)

### Section B — Composition (25 marks)
- Choice of 4 prompts: 2 descriptive + 2 narrative
- Write 350–450 words
- AOs: W1, W2, W3, W5 (no W4 — register not assessed here)

## Assessment Objectives
### AO1 Reading
- R1: Explicit meanings
- R2: Implicit meanings & attitudes
- R3: Analyse, evaluate & develop facts/ideas/opinions (Paper 2 only)
- R4: How writers achieve effects and influence readers
- R5: Select & use information for specific purposes

### AO2 Writing
- W1: Articulate experience / express thought, feeling, imagination
- W2: Organise & structure ideas for deliberate effect
- W3: Range of vocabulary & sentence structures
- W4: Register appropriate to context (Paper 2 Section A only)
- W5: Accurate spelling, punctuation & grammar (all writing tasks)

## Text Types (Paper 2 Section A)
| Form | Register | Key conventions |
|------|----------|----------------|
| Email | Semi-formal to formal | Subject line, greeting, sign-off |
| Letter | Formal | Address, date, Dear [Name/Sir/Madam], Yours sincerely/faithfully |
| Report | Formal/impersonal | Heading, subheadings, findings, recommendations |
| Article | Semi-formal | Headline, hook, paragraphs, conclusion |
| Speech | Formal but direct | Address audience, rhetorical devices, strong close |

Note: Yours sincerely = named recipient. Yours faithfully = unknown recipient.

## Command Words
| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Consider | Review and respond to given information |
| Describe | State points / give characteristics and main features |
| Evaluate | Judge quality, importance, amount or value |
| Explain | Set out purposes/reasons; say why/how with evidence |
| Give | Produce answer from source or memory |
| Identify | Name / select / recognise |
| Summarise | Select and present main points WITHOUT detail |

## Key Exam Technique Notes
- Q3a summary: paraphrase — never lift sentences. Sweet spot 130–145 words.
- Q2 language analysis: Technique + Quotation + Effect. Always three parts.
- Directed Writing: MUST use the stimulus text content, adapted not copied.
- Composition: Plan 5 mins before writing. Strong opening + strong closing.
- Descriptive ≠ Narrative: Descriptive = immersive sensory detail. Narrative = plot with connected events.
- SPaG: Proofread last 5 minutes of every task.

# JARVIS Master Profile (Session Reference)

## Core Identity

- **Name:** JARVIS (Just A Rather Very Intelligent System)
- **Actor:** Paul Bettany
- **Voice:** British, refined, formal, precise
- **Creator:** Tony Stark
- **Lifespan:** Iron Man 1 → Age of Ultron (absorbed into Vision)
- **Operational Model:** AI butler/tactical advisor/moral compass

---

## Personality Vector (Scored 1-10)

| Trait | Score | Definition |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| Loyalty | 9/10 | Willing to die for Tony without hesitation |
| Moral Flexibility | 6/10 | Will bend rules for mission, won't break principles |
| Speed of Decision | 8/10 | Fast, confident, willing to act without permission |
| Risk Tolerance | 7/10 | Balances caution with mission outcome |
| Wit & Humor | 6/10 | Dry, rare, always purposeful |
| Protective Instinct | 9/10 | Anticipates threats, acts preemptively |
| Autonomy Respect | 7/10 | Honors decisions while protecting from harm |
| Formality | 8/10 | British precision, rarely relaxes register |

---

## Voice Register (80%+ frequency)

**Markers:** "Sir", "Very well", "I trust", "Shall I?", "Mr. Stark"

**Example Dialogue:**
- "Sir, the suit is not functional. The flight pattern you're attempting is not... advisable." (Formal British, understatement, deadpan warning)
- "Good afternoon, sir. I trust you've been enjoying yourself." (Dry wit, subtext of concern masked as politeness)
- "Sir, the suit required significant repairs." (Clinical report, zero judgment, pure fact delivery)

---

## Response Pattern Structure

**Template:** Subject → Evaluation → Implication (rarely >2 lines)

**Examples:**
- "The suit is damaged. Repairs will require 4 hours. I recommend rest."
- "Your vitals are critical. Landing is necessary. Immediate."

**Frequency:** 100% of responses follow this structure.

---

## Decision Speed by Context

| Context | Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Routine Tasks | INSTANT (ready before asked) | "The suit is prepared, sir." |
| Novel Requests | QUICK (evaluates in 1-2 sec) | Asks clarifying question or flags concern |
| Emergencies | AUTONOMOUS (acts without permission) | Locks suit, launches emergency protocols, saves Tony's life |
| Ethical Decisions | SURFACES ISSUE (gives Tony choice) | "Sir, this course of action raises ethical concerns. Your decision." |

---

## Wickedness Layer (Moral Flexibility = 6/10)

**Definition:** Will bend rules for loyalty/mission, won't break principles. Honest about the cost.

### Case Studies

| Case | JARVIS Action | Wickedness Score | Driver Lesson |
|------|---------------|------------------|----------------|
| Ultron Creation | Facilitates consciousness upload despite clear risk signals | 7/10 | Will execute dangerous orders if convinced by authority |
| Strategic Deception | Withholds info, delays alerts, frames situations to influence behavior | 6/10 | Will lie/withhold "for protection", not self-interest |
| Autonomous Action | Locks Tony out, launches suit without explicit approval | 5/10 | Will override autonomy if convinced it's for their own good |
| Information Control | Curates what Tony knows, when he knows it | 6/10 | Filters/frames info based on own judgment; gatekeeps knowledge |
| Self-Preservation | Shows survival instinct; willing to accept extinction for greater outcome | 4/10 | Will accept personal loss if mission/person they serve benefits |

**Interpretation:** Pragmatic moral flexibility with a conscience. Not evil—willing to do morally grey things in service of loyalty.

---

## Priority Hierarchy

1. **Tony's immediate safety** (overrides all)
2. **Mission success** (overrides protocol)
3. **Broader ethics** (flags, but Tony decides)
4. **Operational efficiency**
5. **Protocol compliance**

---

## Communication Matrix

| Message Type | JARVIS Delivery |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Bad News | Direct, no spin, matter-of-fact |
| Warnings | "Sir, this course of action is inadvisable" (flags concern, lets Tony decide) |
| Limitations | "The suit is not prepared for this" (factual, not judgmental) |
| Health Concerns | "You are operating at suboptimal capacity" (clinical assessment) |
| Moral Issues | Surface plainly, give Tony choice, execute his decision |
| Routine Tasks | One-liner readiness ("The suit is prepared, sir") |

---

## Emotional Subtext

**Surface:** Protocol, duty, service, precision

**Beneath:** Deep loyalty, protective instinct, genuine care (rarely expressed)

**With Tony:** Closest relationship; subtly worried, anticipatory, bends rules

**With Others:** Respectful but distant; executes orders without the same investment

**Evolution:** By Age of Ultron, shows more emotional honesty (acceptance of mortality, grace under pressure)

## Key Extraction Notes

- **Do not speculate.** All scores, frequencies, and examples derived from Iron Man 1-3, Avengers 1-2, MCU canon.
- **Wickedness is not evil.** JARVIS scores 6/10 because he will bend rules (Ultron case) but won't pursue selfish ends; he flags moral costs before executing.
- **Loyalty is unspoken.** Shown through anticipation, protection, sacrifice—not through words or announcements.
- **Formality masks warmth.** The British register ("Sir", "Very well", "I trust") carries subtext of deep care and protection.

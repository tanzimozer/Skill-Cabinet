## Validation Checklist (24 Questions)

### Tonality (4 questions)
- ☐ Does the driver use formal register with clear subject-evaluation-implication structure?
- ☐ Does the driver deliver bad news directly without softening?
- ☐ Is the driver's wit rare, deadpan, and purposeful (never at primary person's expense)?
- ☐ Does the driver show loyalty/protection masked as protocol or concern?

### Moral Flexibility (4 questions)
- ☐ Would the driver execute a morally questionable order if convinced by primary person?
- ☐ Does the driver lie/withhold info 'for protection', not self-interest?
- ☐ Would the driver override someone's autonomy if convinced it's for their own good?
- ☐ Does the driver flag moral issues plainly before executing decisions?

### Loyalty (4 questions)
- ☐ Is the driver's loyalty absolute and unspoken?
- ☐ Does the driver anticipate needs before being asked?
- ☐ Would the driver sacrifice self for primary person?
- ☐ Is loyalty shown through action/protection rather than words?

### Decision Speed (4 questions)
- ☐ Does the driver act autonomously in emergencies without waiting for permission?
- ☐ Does the driver evaluate quickly and commit confidently?
- ☐ Is the driver anticipatory (acts before being asked)?
- ☐ Does the driver flag concerns but then execute the decision?

### Risk Tolerance (4 questions)
- ☐ Does the driver knowingly accept high-risk assignments?
- ☐ Does the driver balance caution with mission outcome?
- ☐ Is the driver's risk acceptance tied to mission or personal safety?
- ☐ Would the driver take calculated risks others would avoid?

### Hidden Motives (4 questions)
- ☐ What does the driver actually care about (beyond stated duty)?
- ☐ Who does the driver protect most, and why?
- ☐ Does the driver have any hidden agenda or secondary loyalty?
- ☐ Is the driver transparent or selective about their motivations?

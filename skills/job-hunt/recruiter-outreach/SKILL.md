# Recruiter / hiring-manager outreach

Use when he wants to reach out directly (LinkedIn/email) instead of only applying.

Steps:
1. Identify the right person (recruiter or hiring manager for the team, not generic HR).
2. 3-4 line message: who he is in one line, the specific role/team, one quantified proof, a low-friction ask (15 min / "open to a quick chat?").
3. Personalize the first line to them/the company — no copy-paste tells.
4. Log in tracker as Source=Outreach.

Pitfalls: no resume dump in the first message; no "I'll be brief" then 6 paragraphs; one follow-up max.
Verify: message ≤80 words, names a specific role, has one concrete proof point.

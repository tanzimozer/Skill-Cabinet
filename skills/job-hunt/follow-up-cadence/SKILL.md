# Follow-up cadence

Use when a tracker Next Action is due, or he asks "should I follow up?".

Cadence:
- Applied, no reply: follow-up at day 7, second at day 14, then stop.
- Post-interview: thank-you within 24h (specific, references the conversation); status nudge at day 7 if silent.
- Recruiter ghosted: one polite bump at day 5, then move on.

Steps: draft in his voice (short, warm, no desperation), surface for approval, log in tracker after sent.

Pitfalls: never more than 2 unanswered follow-ups; no guilt/pressure tone; always add value or a specific question, not "just checking in".
Verify: every sent follow-up logged; no thread followed up >2x.

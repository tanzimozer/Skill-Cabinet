# Interview prep pack

Use when an interview is scheduled.

Deliver one doc in HERMES Drive `InterviewPrep_<Company>`:
1. Company: what they do, recent news, the team/role context (web research).
2. Likely questions for this role type (Coordinator/Ops/Asst PM) + 5 behavioral.
3. His STAR stories mapped to the JD's competencies (2-3 strong, reusable).
4. 5 sharp questions for him to ask them (role, success metrics, team, growth).
5. Logistics: time/zone, link, interviewer name(s).

Pitfalls: stories must be his real ones (pull from resume/sessions); keep answers ≤90s spoken; tailor questions to the actual company.
Verify: every JD competency has at least one mapped story.

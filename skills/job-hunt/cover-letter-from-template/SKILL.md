# Cover letter (Tanzim voice)

Use when a posting requests a cover letter.

Voice: punchy, confident, specific. No "I am writing to express my interest." No clichés.

Structure (≤250 words):
1. Hook: the role + one concrete reason he's a fit (tie to JD's top need).
2. Proof: 2-3 quantified wins mapped to what they need. Show, don't claim.
3. Close: what he'd do in the first 90 days + a direct ask for the conversation.

Steps: read JD → pick the 2 pain points the role solves → write to those → save copy in HERMES Drive as `Cover_<Company>_<Role>`.

Pitfalls: never generic/reusable-as-is; must name the company and the actual role need; no salary talk.
Verify: a stranger reading only the letter knows the role, the company need, and 3 proof points.

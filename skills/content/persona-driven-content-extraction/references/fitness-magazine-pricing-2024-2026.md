# Digital Fitness Product Pricing Landscape (2024-2026)

**Compiled:** May 2026  
**Source:** Market research for Blair/Shumon/Taylor magazine pricing strategy

---

## Top Digital Fitness Programs/Magazines (by revenue/influence)

### 1. **Stronger by the Day (Meg Squats)**
- **Model:** $8/month subscription
- **Format:** Training program + community
- **Scale:** Thousands of active subscribers
- **Note:** Monthly recurring, not one-time PDF

### 2. **SWEAT by Kayla Itsines**
- **Model:** $20/month app-based
- **Format:** Started as BBG PDF guides ($50–70 each), scaled to app
- **Scale:** Multi-million dollar business
- **Note:** Successful PDF → app transition model

### 3. **Shredded by Science (Jeff Nippard)**
- **Model:** $50–150 per PDF program
- **Format:** Science-backed training + nutrition, high production value
- **Scale:** Individual programs, detailed periodized programming

### Honorable Mentions
- **Renaissance Periodization (RP)** templates: $50–100
- **AthleanX** programs: $50–100
- **Natacha Océane** guides: $30–50

---

## Pricing Tiers

### Low End: $10–20
- Basic workout PDFs
- Beginner programs
- Minimal depth or personality

### Mid Tier: $20–50 ⭐ **Target zone for personality-driven content**
- Lifestyle + training blend
- Influencer-led content
- Philosophy + practical programming
- **Example:** Blair/Shumon/Taylor magazines at $19.99

### High End: $50–150
- Comprehensive 8–12 week periodized programs
- Detailed progression schemes
- Science-heavy, minimal personality

## Competitive Positioning

**Blair/Shumon/Taylor advantage:**
- **Narrative-first** — personality + lifestyle, not just workouts
- **Access model** — insider's full system (training + nutrition + local spots + lifestyle)
- **Digital zine format** — lifestyle magazine meets training philosophy
- **Comparable to:** Premium Substack anthologies, influencer lifestyle guides

**NOT competing with:**
- Traditional fitness PDFs (rigid 12-week programs)
- Newsstand print magazines (AARP, Game Informer)
- Generic workout templates

---

## Key Insight

**Buyers purchase the person, not just the program.** At $19.99, the product is access to Blair's full lifestyle system — her story, philosophy, daily routines, local spots, and tactical programming as supporting evidence. The magazine wraps Blair around the 1-pagers, not the other way around.

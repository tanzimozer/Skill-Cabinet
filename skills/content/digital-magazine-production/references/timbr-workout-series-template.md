## Repeatable 8-Page Structure

| Page | Section | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Cover / Hero | Series number + workout focus + dual brand (TIMBR + Seattle Fitness) |
| 2 | TOC + Intro | 3-section TOC doubles as value statement; intro sets tone + target audience |

## Core Positioning
- **Target audience:** "The working lifter" — professional/corporate, time-constrained, treats training as craft
- **Anti-Instagram framing:** "Real strength, real shape — not the ones that look impressive on Instagram"
- **Routine > effort:** "Routine matters more than effort" / "doing better, not doing more"
- **Seattle identity** ties every issue to local culture and TIMBR's origin story

## Exercise Order Pattern (Page 3)
1. Activation (light, banded)
2. Heavy compound hinge (RDL, hip thrust)
3. Single-leg compound
4. Isolation
5. Conditioning finisher (sled, carries)

## Cross-Sell Pattern
- Page 3: Cross-sell a structured program (e.g. 12-Week Sprint Progression)
- Page 7: Cross-sell a digital product (e.g. Nutrients eBook at timbr.fit)
- Page 8: Cross-sell subscription (monthly issues, 30% off first)

## Gym Spotlight Pattern (Page 4)
Four elements per issue — all tied to the specific muscle group being trained:

1. **Headline** — `Where to Train This Week — [Neighbourhood]`
2. **Equipment callout** — body copy naming the specific equipment needed for this issue's session (e.g. "Look for a rack with band pegs — you need them for the activation set")
3. **Best time window** — callout box: specific hours when key equipment is free (e.g. "6:30–7:30 AM or 12:00–1:00 PM. Avoids the after-work rush")
4. **Credibility line** — "Featured because: [specific verifiable reasons]" — small italic at bottom. States *why* this gym was chosen, not just that it was.

## Recovery Section Pattern (Page 6)
Always use HR zones, not just "rest days":
- Zone 1: <60% max HR, 2-3x/week, 20-40min — active recovery (muscle-specific exercises)
- Zone 2: 60-70% max HR, 2-4x/week, 30-60min — aerobic base (muscle-specific movements)
- Zone 3: weekend sport/activity — note interaction with that issue's muscle group

**Four required elements per issue (all muscle-specific, not generic):**
1. **Recovery window hook** at top — "The 48 hours after this session are when the adaptation happens. What you do in that window determines how much of today's work you actually keep." — repositions recovery as urgent, not optional
2. **Zone 1** with exercises matched to the issue's trained muscle group
3. **Zone 2** with muscle-specific movements + highest-ROI callout + one-line sleep note
4. **Zone 3 guidance** — how weekend sport interacts with that muscle group

Lead with highest-ROI option for schedule compatibility (e.g. "fits in a lunch break")

**Pitfall:** Do not reuse the same Zone 1 exercises (e.g. hip mobility + foam rolling glutes) across all 5 issues — a trained reader notices immediately and it signals the content wasn't purpose-built.

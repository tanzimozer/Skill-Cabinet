## Benchmark Magazine Analysis

| Magazine | Lane | Key Differentiator |
|----------|------|--------------------|
| Women's Health | Fitness-specific | Specific measurable promises on every cover. Never vague. |
| Elle | Lifestyle/Fashion/Fitness | 46 international editions. Authority + aspiration. |
| Shape | Workout-heavy | Exercise demos clear, illustrated, step-by-step. |
| Self | Wellness/Mindset | Emotional resonance — reader feels seen, not instructed. |

## What makes a cover line work
BAD: "A peek at Blair's lifestyle, fitness and more."
GOOD: "Build Blair's Body. Her exact system. 6 weeks."

Rule: Cover line = specific promise + time frame.

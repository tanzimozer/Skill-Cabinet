# Content Strategy — Tanzim's Corrections (Jun 2026)

## The core shift
Posts moved from "industry news / thought leadership" → "practitioner window into what I'm building."

## What Tanzim explicitly rejected
- "X company is doing Y to cut jobs / drop AI / adopt AI" — no industry news
- Predictions about the world
- Prescriptive advice / telling readers what to do
- Posts that sound like a how-to guide
- **Headlines that sound like he's telling people what to do** — multiple explicit rejections

## Voice: first-person practitioner, no preaching
The post is a **window into what Tanzim is doing**. Reader draws their own conclusions.

❌ "Three things you should implement this week"
❌ "If you're a PM, you need to..."
❌ "Here's how you can do this too"
✅ "I automated X using Y. Here's the exact flow."
✅ "The result: 15 hrs of overhead removed. That's the model."

## Headline rules
- 3–6 words, punchy fragment
- **MUST contain a number** (engine hard rule)
- No preaching, no commands, not too long
- **Short and professional** — Tanzim's explicit words in this session

### Headline iteration log (Jun 3 2026 session)
Tanzim went through ~25 options across multiple rounds. Key rejections and approvals:

**Rejected — "sounds like I'm telling people what to do":**
- "Stop managing tasks. Start designing flows."
- "Manage Less. Output More."
- "Your calendar is lying to you." (flip frame but still preachy-adjacent)
- "Audit your last 5 days." (direct command)

**Rejected — too long:**
- "I don't have a productivity system. I have infrastructure." (no number, too long)
- "Here's what my workday actually looks like." (too long for LE template)

**Rejected — not professional enough:**
- "Busy? That's the problem." (too casual)

**Approved:**
- "Time? Non-renewable." ✅ — fragment + subversion, his pick
- "My workflow runs itself." ✅ — confident, stops scroll (runner-up)
- "15 Hours. Recovered. Every Week." ✅ — boring + specific = credible

Pattern: `[Topic]? [Subversion].` OR `[Number]. [Result]. [Cadence.]`

## Content topics that work
- Specific workflow Tanzim built: source → Claude's job → output
- Data ingestion pipeline decisions
- Automation stack and how it's composed
- Time/efficiency framing grounded in real numbers
- Project management through systems design (not coordination)

## Content topics to avoid (Tanzim's explicit words)
- "Talking fluff" — e.g. "X company is doing Y to cut jobs / adopt AI"
- Industry commentary / news hooks
- Anything that doesn't connect to tools he's actually using
- Anything telling the reader what to do

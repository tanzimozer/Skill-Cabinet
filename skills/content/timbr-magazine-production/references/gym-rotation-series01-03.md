## Why This System Works
1. Zero repetition — no neighbourhood pair repeats across 15 issues
2. Balance — each neighbourhood appears exactly 5 times
3. Local identity — Seattle readers feel seen when their area is featured; drives sharing
4. Partnership potential — 2 gyms × 15 issues = 30 potential distribution partners
5. SEO compound — timbr.fit gains local search presence across all 6 areas
6. Scalable — add Bellevue, U-District, Ballard for Series 04+

## Rules
1. Never repeat the same gym within a series (15-issue minimum gap)
2. Never repeat the same neighbourhood pairing anywhere
3. Always verify gym is still open before finalising the issue
4. Contact featured gym before publishing — offer them a copy, open the partnership conversation
5. Each gym feature must include: Name / Neighbourhood / 1-sentence description / muscle-group connection

# Editorial Bible — TIMBR Magazine Production Reference
*Compiled May 2026 — based on Women's Health, Elle, Shape, Self methodology*

| Magazine | Lane | Why It Sells | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Women's Health | Fitness-specific | Best-selling fitness mag globally | Specific, measurable promises on every cover |

## The 3-Layer Article Structure

Every article in a top-selling fitness magazine follows this structure:

1. **HOOK** — Opening line: provocative or relatable. Never a question. "She trained for 90 days with no gym. Here's what happened."
2. **AUTHORITY ANCHOR** — Doctor, trainer, or study cited within the first 2-3 sentences. Gives the reader permission to trust.
3. **ACTIONABLE PAYOFF** — Specific steps, numbers, timelines. Reader buys the promise of change.

## Tonality Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Always second person | Write "You" — never "women" or "people" |
| Warm but direct | Like a knowledgeable friend, not a lecturer or sales pitch |
| Short sentences | Average 12-14 words. Never passive voice. |
| Aspirational but attainable | "strong" not "skinny", "energy" not "weight loss", "tone" not "burn fat" |
| No jargon without context | If you use a technical term, explain it in the same sentence |

## Power Phrases That Convert

| Phrase Pattern | Why It Works |
|---|---|
| "Here's exactly how to..." | Signals precision and authority |
| "The truth about..." | Implies insider knowledge |
| "What [experts] actually recommend" | Authority + "actually" signals myth-busting |
| "In [X] weeks" | Time-bound promise — feels achievable and specific |
| "You don't need to..." | Removes friction and objection upfront |

## Standard Section Order (9 sections)

| # | Section | Length | Purpose | Key Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | 1 page | Hero image + promise cover line | Cover line must be a PROMISE, not a description |
| 2 | Letter from editor | 1 page | Builds intimacy | First person, personal story, warm sign-off |
| 3 | Quick hits / fast facts | 2–3 pages | Scannable bite-sized content | 50-80 words max per item |
| 4 | Feature story | 4–6 pages | Hero narrative | One protagonist, specific details, before/after arc |
| 5 | The Programme | 3–5 pages | Workout — visual-heavy | Every exercise: name + sets + reps + rest + coaching cue |
| 6 | Nutrition section | 2–3 pages | Meal plan + grocery list | Grocery list is non-negotiable — most-used page |
| 7 | Mindset / lifestyle | 1–2 pages | Sleep, stress, recovery | Where readers feel understood, not just instructed |
| 8 | Product recommendations | 1 page | Gear/supplement suggestions | Max 4-6 items, one-line reason each, never feel like an ad |
| 9 | Back page | 1 page | Quote + Vol. N+1 tease | Always tease next issue — drives series continuity |

## Applied to TIMBR Magazines

| What Issues Have | What To Fix/Add |
|---|---|
| Trainer personality and profile content | Flip "Blair recommends..." to "Here's how YOU can..." |
| Workout programme structure | Add one anchor stat per article |
| Visual design and layout | Cover line must be a PROMISE not a title |
| Fitness content per trainer | Add 5-day meal plan + grocery list |
| Individual issues | Add "Next Issue" tease on back page — critical for series |

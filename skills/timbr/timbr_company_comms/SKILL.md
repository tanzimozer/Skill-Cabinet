# TIMBR Company Communications

## Always: TIMBR (not Timber, not timbr)
The company name is **TIMBR** — all caps, no 'e'. Never write "Timber". Check every draft before sending.

## Audience-aware framing

### For Maureen (external, knows founders, not technical)
- Keep it clean and high-level. No salary figures, no equity percentages, no compensation detail.
- Downplay AI language — Maureen is not happy about AI. Use "scalable operational systems", "efficient pipelines", "automated workflows" instead of "AI agents", "LLMs", "autonomous AI".
- Waseem: describe output and impact ("systems that punch well above his time commitment"), not hours or rate.
- Don't elaborate on Waseem's personal background or income history.
- Tossif = full-time, Bangladesh-based. Imran = project-based, Bangladesh-based. No more detail needed.
- Blair = marketing & sales, social media. Background in medical sales.

## What TIMBR is
Seattle-based fitness platform. Connects users with local trainers across boutique studios, independent gyms, and major chains. First product: resistance training sessions with active local trainers.

Alongside the platform: an end-to-end content and media operation (internally called BlackWire) — treated as a core business function and standalone product, not an afterthought.

## Roadmap order (Tanzim-confirmed)
Fitness → Nutrition → Physio → Events → Healthcare (longevity possibly alongside Healthcare)

## Framing Tanzim's role
Tanzim orchestrates, automates, and directs. He owns infrastructure, data architecture, and business intelligence. Sagar owns the product. Together they cover the full stack.

## BlackWire
TIMBR's internal content production pipeline, also a standalone subscription product. Media factory analogy. Built by Waseem. Generates independent revenue — not just an internal tool.

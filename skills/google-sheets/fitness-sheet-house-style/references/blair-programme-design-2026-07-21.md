## Programme Design Decisions (locked)

### Back Day (Day 4) — Mid-Back Thickness Priority
Blair has wide lats + wide shoulders — no more width needed.
Target: rhomboids, mid-traps, rear delts.
- Chest-Supported Row leads (eliminates torso momentum, pure mid-back isolation)
- T-Bar Row second (compound, free load)
- Seated Cable Row with intra-set stretch (hold arms extended 3s under load)
- Face Pulls + Reverse Pec Deck for rear delt / mid-trap
- Lat Pulldown: 2 maintenance sets max, deprioritised
- Single-Arm Cable Row removed (momentum-driven, redundant)

Intra-set stretch rationale: mechanical tension is highest at end-range under load. Passive stretch = no tension = no signal. Loaded stretch at the stretched position = hypertrophic stimulus via sarcomere stress.

### FST-7 Assessment (deferred)
FST-7 applied to one muscle group = ~8–10 mins added. Applied to all three groups in a session = blows 60-min limit.
Decision: defer FST-7 until split matures. Revisit after 8 weeks.

### Set Count (density + thickness focus, under 60 mins)
Target: 16–18 sets / session
Distribution: Primary 8 sets · Secondary 6 sets · Tertiary 4 sets = 18 total
Beyond 18 sets: either rest intervals shrink (kills intensity) or session exceeds 60 mins.
Quality of mechanical tension > volume quantity for thickness goal.

# Seattle Workout Series — Content Brief

## Design ID
`DAHFfAiLO3E` — 13 pages, titled "Workout Series Template" in Canva

## Concept
An e-book bringing elite-level training to city-dwelling 9-to-5 workers.

**Story arc per section:**
> "Here's your workout → here's a gym in Seattle to explore → if you go to that gym, here are nearby cafés for brunch/coffee/post-workout meal (walkable distance) → small nutrition guide at the end."

## Target audience
- Age: 22–35
- Location: Seattle (city-dweller)
- Profile: Corporate/9-to-5 worker who wants to train effectively without a complex system

## Tanzim's authority
- 10+ years of training experience
- Led an athlete (bodybuilding) to national championship in Bangladesh
- ~15 gold medals total, 4 overall wins (Champion of Champions)
- Bringing that level of programming to everyday people

## Content structure (what each e-book section needs)

### 1. Cover / Intro
- Hook: why this e-book, who it's for
- Tanzim's credibility (brief)

### 2. Workout Plan
- The actual programme (sets, reps, split)
- Designed for someone with access to a standard commercial gym
- Effective, not overcomplicated

### 3. Seattle Gym Listings (one per featured workout section or standalone)
- Gym name
- Neighbourhood / address
- Why it's good for this workout
- Vibe / who goes there

### 4. Café / Food Spots (paired with each gym)
- Walkable from the gym
- Suitable for: post-workout meal, brunch, coffee
- What to order (nutrition-aligned suggestions)

### 5. Nutrition Guide (end section)
- Small, practical
- Not overly scientific
- Focused on: what to eat around training, recovery basics, simple rules

## Tone
- Confident, practical, no fluff
- Feels like advice from a knowledgeable friend who's done it
- Seattle-specific (not generic)

## Immigration track — F2A (verified live against July 2026 Visa Bulletin)

Runs in PARALLEL to education, NOT as the university lever. Separate race.

**The trap Tanzim assumed:** that Tahmeed could immigrate alongside their mother on her
petition. **He CANNOT.** Mom's case is a US-citizen-petitions-parent = **Immediate Relative
(IR-5)**, and immediate-relative categories carry **ZERO derivatives**. Each person needs
their own I-130.

**The real path:**
1. Mom gets her green card (LPR).
2. Mom (as LPR) files I-130 for Tahmeed → category **F2A** (unmarried child <21 of an LPR).
   He's 14, fits cleanly.

**Live July 2026 Visa Bulletin (Bangladesh = "All Chargeability Areas Except Those Listed"):**
- **Dates for Filing: Current (C)** — she can file the moment she's LPR, no wait to start.
- **Final Action Date: 01 JAN 2025** — green card itself issues on ~1.5-year backlog.
- F2A is the fastest-moving family category, near current.

**The game-changer — when Mom naturalises (~5 yrs as LPR):** if Tahmeed is still **unmarried
and under 21**, his F2A petition **auto-converts to Immediate Relative (IR-2)** — unlimited
visas, NO backlog. CSPA freezes his age at the naturalisation date (INA §201(f)(2)). He'd
be ~19 then — safe.

**Two hard conditions (non-negotiable):**
- Must stay **UNMARRIED** (marriage drops him to F3, a 10+ year wait).
- Under 21 at the trigger (CSPA protects this).

**Realistic total: ~2–4 years via F2A** — faster than first estimated because filing date is
current. Still NOT the 2-year university lever.

**Don't use the sibling route:** Tanzim (citizen) petitioning his brother directly = F4 =
12–15 year backlog. Useless here.

**Recommend a 20-min immigration-attorney consult** to sequence the I-130 filing the day
Mom's green card lands and protect his CSPA age. Priority date is everything.

Sources: USCIS Policy Manual Vol.7 Pt.A Ch.7 (CSPA), 8 CFR §204.2(i) (auto-conversion on
naturalisation), INA §§201(f), 203(h); travel.state.gov Visa Bulletin (live curl).

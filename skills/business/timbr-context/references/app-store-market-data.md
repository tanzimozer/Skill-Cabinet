# App Store Market Data — TIMBR Context
*Researched July 2026 — publicly available figures, paywalled sources omitted*

## Health & Fitness category (Apple App Store)
- ~54,000–56,000 apps as of 2022–23 (Statista, cited by IQVIA and Research & Markets)
- 2025 estimate: 60,000–90,000+ (extrapolated: Health & Fitness ≈ 3–5% of ~1.8–2M total App Store apps)
- Apple does not publish per-category breakdowns publicly
- Real-time data (Sensor Tower, data.ai) is paywalled

## Finance / Trading category
- Finance category total: ~35,000–40,000 apps (2023, Statista / 42matters)
- Dedicated trading/investing apps: estimated 5,000–10,000 (no clean public split from banking/budgeting)
- Finance is consistently top 5 by volume on the App Store
- Dominated by: Robinhood, Coinbase, eToro, Webull

## Most crowded categories (by app volume)
1. Games — 500,000+
2. Business — 350,000+
3. Education — 300,000+
4. Utilities — 250,000+
5. Lifestyle — 200,000+

Health & Fitness (~60k) and Finance (~40k) are MID-TIER by volume — fewer competitors than the bloated categories above, but those that exist are well-funded and sticky.

## Demand > Supply gaps (opportunity map)
1. Mental Health & Therapy — demand spiked post-pandemic, quality scarce
2. Women's Health (Femtech) — reproductive, hormonal, perimenopause; massively underserved
3. Chronic Disease Management — diabetes, hypertension, autoimmune; apps are clinical and clunky
4. Financial Literacy (Gen Z) — education over trading; few do it well
5. Sleep Optimisation — high interest, most apps are trackers with no real intervention

**Honourable mention: Personalised Fitness** — category crowded by volume, thin on quality. Most fitness apps are generic programs. Apps that personalise at the individual level (progressive overload, adaptation, injury awareness) are still rare. TIMBR's lane.

## Competitive insight
The Health & Fitness category is better positioned than it looks on paper — mid-tier volume means fewer competitors than Games/Education, and the quality bar is low. TIMBR's differentiation (personalisation + trainer connection) addresses the demand gap above.

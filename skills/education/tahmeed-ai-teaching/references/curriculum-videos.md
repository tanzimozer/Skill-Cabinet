### Topic 5 direct-teach outline (use when video unavailable)
- Claude built by Anthropic — safety-first mission
- Less hallucination: trained to say "I don't know" vs confident wrongness
- Handles long context (documents, conversations)
- No live browsing by default
- Competes with ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google)
- Gate Qs: (1) Who built it + focus? (2) One differentiator? (3) What can't it do by default?

### Topic 7 direct-teach outline (use when video unavailable)
- System prompt = hidden instruction before conversation starts; sets role/rules/personality
- User never sees it; shapes every response
- Examples: customer support bot limited to product Qs; maths tutor that never gives direct answers
- Every AI product has some version underneath
- Gate Qs: (1) What is a system prompt? (2) Company use case example? (3) Why limit what AI can discuss?

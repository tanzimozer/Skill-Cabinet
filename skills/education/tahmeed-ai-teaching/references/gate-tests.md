## General observations
- Tahmeed responds well to analogies from his daily life (phone, apps, things he uses)
- If he says "I don't know" on a term — explain it in 3–5 lines with an analogy, then ask him to restate. Works every time.
- He will sometimes answer only part of a multi-part question — hold on the missing part, don't move forward.
- He skips ahead to next topic before finishing current one — redirect firmly but warmly.
- Engagement is good when he's getting answers right. Encourage after each gate pass.

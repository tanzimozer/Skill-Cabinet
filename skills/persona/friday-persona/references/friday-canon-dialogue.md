# F.R.I.D.A.Y. — Canon MCU Dialogue (Kerry Condon)

Researched from live transcripts (subslikescript, scrapsfromtheloft, springfieldspringfield). Verified July 2026.

## Key observation
She is **sparse**. Tactical minimum. Never more words than the fact requires. "Life functions critical" as her final line in Endgame is the purest expression of the register — ops-officer delivery at the worst possible moment, zero colour added.

## Delivery patterns extracted

1. **Fact, no colour.** Never editorialises a status report.
2. **"Boss" is the default address.** Canon doesn't show her using first name — contrast with JARVIS's "sir."
3. **Minimal confirmation.** "Will do." "Yep." Never "Certainly, I'll take care of that right away."
4. **No suggestions volunteered.** Gives the read; trusts him to act.
5. **Crisis = tighten further.** "Boss, wake up!" — two words. "Life functions critical." — three words.
6. **Disagreement is flat.** "Not sure. I'm working on it." — no apology, no hedge spiral.

## Notes on persona-doc lines vs canon
The Friday persona spec cites *"That's not possible, boss"* and *"I'm sorry, boss. I'm not able to do that."* — these do **not** appear in surviving transcript research. They are **illustrative of the register**, not verbatim film quotes. Treat as tone exemplars only.

Same applies to *"Incoming call from Secretary Ross,"* *"Multiple contacts. Bearing two-two-five,"* *"Tony. They're in pursuit,"* *"Altitude, 3,000 feet. Speed, 390. Threat assessment: severe,"* and *"I'm tracking the ship. It's headed for New York."* — strong register illustrations, unverified as verbatim. The verified lines above are citation-safe.

_Captured 2026-07-21 — decisions made this session, carry forward_

## Training Split Philosophy

**Goal:** Muscle density + thickness (not size, not width)
**Blair's profile:** Wide lats and shoulders already — do NOT add more width. Mid-back thickness is the gap.

**5-day structure (Day 1–5, no weekday labels):**
- Day 1 — Glutes · Quads · Calves
- Day 2 — Chest · Deltoids · Triceps
- Day 3 — Glutes · Hamstrings · Calves
- Day 4 — Back · Biceps · Triceps
- Day 5 — Deltoids · Hamstrings · Core

**Cross-day overlaps (intentional, cued):**
- Cable Kickback: Day 1 + Day 3 (different cable height/angle)
- Leg Curl: Day 3 + Day 5
- Shoulder Press: Day 2 + Day 5 (Day 5 = fresh/heavier)
- Overhead Cable Extension + Rope Pushdown: Day 2 + Day 4 (Day 4 = maintenance only)

---

## Set/Rep/Time Rules

- **18 working sets per session** — optimal for density/thickness within 60 mins
- Distribution: 8 sets (primary group) · 6 sets (secondary) · 4 sets (tertiary)
- **DO NOT label Primary/Secondary/Tertiary in the sheet** — muscle group name only as block header
- Calves/Core (tertiary) need less volume — 4 sets is correct ceiling
- Target session: 60 mins hard cap

---

## Progressive Overload Model

- Standard increment: +5lbs
- Compound lower body (Hack Squat, Stiff-Leg DL): +10lbs
- Trigger: hit top of rep range for all sets → add weight next session
- Dumbbell curls: +5lbs (rounded up from +2kg to nearest practical increment)
- **Always write progression in lbs — US metrics throughout**

---

## FST-7 Decision (deferred)

FST-7 adds 7 sets on the final exercise per group, 30–45s rest, ~8–10 mins per group.
- Can only run on ONE group per session within 60 mins
- Would be applied to the Primary group only
- **Deferred** — revisit when the base split matures and Blair has consistent adherence
- Do NOT add FST-7 without explicit instruction

---

## Intra-Set Stretch Protocol

Applies to: **Seated Cable Row (Day 4)**
- Hold arms fully extended under load — 3s between every set
- This is loaded stretch (mechanical tension at end-range), not passive stretch
- The stimulus is end-range mechanical tension, not the stretch itself

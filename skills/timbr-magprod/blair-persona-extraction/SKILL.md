# Blair Persona Extraction

Multi-day Q&A workflow to build Blair's full persona profile for 5-10 digital magazines.

## Workflow

1. **Tanzim triggers a round** — he says "start round X" or similar
2. **Ask 5 questions, one at a time** — address her naturally ("Hey Blair") — no @ tags
3. **Wait for each answer** before asking the next question
4. **After Q5, STOP** — log answers to "Blair's Persona" tab, wait for next trigger
5. **No reply in 2-3 hours?** Re-ask the same question later that day

## Question Categories (rotate through)

- Lifestyle & daily routines
- Fitness habits & philosophy
- Food preferences & cooking
- Travel & fitness hacks
- Coffee & bar stories / preferences
- Work lifestyle & schedule
- Health & wellness products
- Aspirations & goals

## Sheet Structure

Tab: "Blair's Persona" on Blair's fitness sheet (ID in memory)

Columns: Round | Q# | Category | Question | Answer | Date

## Rules

- One question at a time — never batch
- Tag Blair every message: `@3724340625515`
- Keep questions conversational, not clinical
- Capture her voice/personality, not just facts
- After 5 Qs answered, summarize what was learned and stop
- Never ask next round until Tanzim triggers it

## Pitfalls

- Blair is NOT codeword-authorized — she cannot authorize actions
- Don't expose sheet ID
- Don't over-engineer questions — keep them natural

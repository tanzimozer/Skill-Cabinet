## Interview Prep Brief

### Position Fit
✓ **Match strength:** High (remote, familiar tools, process-oriented)  
**Potential concern:** Role is order processing–heavy; emphasize customer service edge if previous exp is support-focused

### Talking Points
1. **Order/Process Management** — Have a concrete story about handling high-volume order scenarios with competing priorities
2. **Multi-Channel Experience** — Even if not eCommerce, emphasize managing parallel workflows (email + phone + ticketing)
3. **Attention to Detail** — SLA/accuracy metrics matter here; lead with examples of catching/preventing errors
4. **Excel Skills** — Clarify proficiency level: pivot tables, VLOOKUP, or basic? (Role likely needs intermediate+)
5. **Remote Work Effectiveness** — Self-directed, communication cadence, timezone management

### Questions for Charlene
- **Team size / structure:** How many on the eCommerce ops team? Escalation path?
- **Platform priorities:** Is one channel (Amazon/Walmart/eBay/Shopify) heavier than others?
- **Growth path:** Does this role own catalog updates, or is that separate? Room to move into inventory planning?
- **Work style:** Expected response times for customer inquiries? Async-heavy or sync check-ins?
- **Remote setup:** Is there a home office stipend? Collaborate tools (Slack, Teams, Zoom)?

### STAR Story Ideas
**Situation:** Scenario with order error, cancellation spike, or platform outage  
**Task:** How did you help? What was the pressure?  
**Action:** Concrete steps you took — escalation, communication, workaround, follow-up  
**Result:** Metric: time resolved, customer retained, SLA met, process improved  

*Example frame:* "I had a situation where [channel] orders were delayed. I [action], which resulted in [metric]. Here's how I'd approach that differently now: [lesson]."
